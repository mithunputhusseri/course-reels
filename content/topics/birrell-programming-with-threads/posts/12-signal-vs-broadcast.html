Wake policy

Signal vs Broadcast

Signal

At most one waiter can usefully proceed (e.g. last reader leaves → one writer).

Broadcast

Several waiters may proceed, or you group wait reasons on one condition for simpler code.

  • With predicate loops, Broadcast may replace Signal safely — only cost is extra wake-ups
  • Often better: decide under the lock, Signal after unlock (avoids wake-then-block on m)
Birrell's favorite micro-opt: move Signal outside the LOCK when no protected state is needed afterward.
