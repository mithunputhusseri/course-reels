Golden rule

Always WHILE, never IF

LOCK m DO
  WHILE head = NIL DO
    Thread.Wait(m, nonEmpty)
  END;
  topElement := head;
  head := head^.next;
END;
  • Signal doesn't hand you the mutex next — another consumer may steal the item
  • SRC may awaken extra waiters for efficiency
  • The loop makes the postcondition locally obvious
Mesa-style (SRC): Signal is a hint to recheck. Hoare's stronger handoff could skip the retest — SRC prefers simpler impl + cheap WHILE.
