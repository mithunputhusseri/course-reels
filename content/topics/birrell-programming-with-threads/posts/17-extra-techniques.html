Extra toolkit

Up-calls, stamps & work crews

Up-calls

Network packet climbs layers in one thread (no switch per layer). Don't hold a mutex across an up-call.

Version stamps

Bump a counter when authoritative data changes; callers pass the stamp; mismatch → retry with fresh data.

Work crews

Too much parallelism? Fixed worker pool + job queue — not one Fork per tiny task.

Embarrassment of parallelism: forking every statement/procedure can drown the scheduler — crews bound concurrency.
