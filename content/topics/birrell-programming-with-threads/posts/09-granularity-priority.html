Performance

Granularity & priority inversion

Coarse lock

Simple, fewer missed syncs. Unrelated ops serialize → contention.

Fine locks

More parallelism (e.g. global open-table + per-file mutex). More proof burden.

  • Don't hold a mutex across slow I/O / page faults if you can avoid it
  • Priority inversion: high waits on lock held by low, while medium runs forever
  • Systemic fix: priority inheritance (SRC didn't provide it)
Birrell: locking as simple as possible — but no simpler.
