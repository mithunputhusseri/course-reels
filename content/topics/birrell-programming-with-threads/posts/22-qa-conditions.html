Practice · Q5–Q6

Conditions & Mesa style

Q5

Why is Wait's unlock+block atomic? What race does it prevent?

A: Prevents a signal between deciding to sleep and sleeping (wake-up-waiting race) — the wake would be lost.

Q6

Why WHILE not IF after Wait? How do SRC semantics differ from Hoare's?

A: Another thread may take the resource; Signal may wake extras. SRC/Mesa = hint + recheck; Hoare handoff could skip retest.

Check: Broadcast can replace Signal for correctness if every waiter uses a predicate loop.
