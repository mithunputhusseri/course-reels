Practice · Q7–Q8

Deadlock & nested monitors

Q7

How does a partial lock order prevent A/B circular deadlock on M1 and M2?

A: Every thread that needs both acquires them in the same order, so a wait cycle can't form.

Q8

In nested Get/Give, Get Waits on b while holding a. Why can't Give unblock it?

A: Wait released only b; Get still holds a. Give needs a before it can set ready and Signal.

Remedy: end the outer LOCK before calling a blocking lower layer.
