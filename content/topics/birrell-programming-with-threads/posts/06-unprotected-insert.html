The unprotected Insert bug

Without a lock, two threads can both write table[i] before either increments i:

(* BAD — no LOCK *)
table[i] := r;   (* 1 *)
i := i + 1;      (* 2 *)
  • One value is lost (overwritten)
  • Two increments leave an orphaned NIL slot
  • Timing depends on page faults, timers, true MP asynchrony
(* FIXED *)
LOCK m DO
  table[i] := r;
  i := i + 1;
END;
Birrell prefers: an obvious deadlock over a silent wrong answer.
