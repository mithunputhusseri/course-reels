Practice · Q5–Q6

Lifecycle & scheduling

Q5

A process issues a disk read. Which state does it enter? What event returns it to ready?

A: Waiting (blocked). I/O completion moves it back to ready.

Q6

If \(T_p = T_s\), roughly what CPU efficiency do you get? Why is that bad?

A: ~50%. Half the time is switch/scheduler overhead — too much thrashing relative to useful work.

Check: only ready processes are dispatch candidates; efficiency ≈ \(T_p/(T_p+T_s)\).
