Protection

Page tables keep memory simple and safe

  • Address space — in-memory view of a process
  • Sparse layout — not all of V0–Vmax is mapped; gaps are normal
  • Demand paging — pages may live in RAM or on disk until used
  • Protection — OS checks whether an access is allowed
Key: each process thinks it owns a private contiguous space; the MMU + page tables make that illusion real.
