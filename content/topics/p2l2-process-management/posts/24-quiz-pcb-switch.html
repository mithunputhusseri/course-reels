Practice · Q3–Q4

PCB & context switch

Q3

While P1 runs, where do its register values live? After a switch to P2, where is P1's state?

A: In the CPU while running; saved into PCB.P1 when preempted.

Q4

Name one direct and one indirect cost of a context switch.

A: Direct: register save/restore cycles. Indirect: cold cache / TLB misses / pipeline flush.

Check: save → schedule → restore → resume.
