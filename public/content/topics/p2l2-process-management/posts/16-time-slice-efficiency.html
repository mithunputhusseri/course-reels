Efficiency

Time slice vs switch overhead

Useful work \(T_p\) vs scheduler/switch cost \(T_s\):

efficiency ≈ Tp / (Tp + Ts)
  • \(T_p = 10×T_s\) — ~91% useful
  • \(T_p = T_s\) — ~50% (too much overhead)
  • \(T_p ≫ T_s\) — efficient, but worse interactive latency
Tradeoff: short slices → snappy UI, more switches; long slices → efficient, less interactive.
