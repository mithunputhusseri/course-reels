Practice · Q9–Q10

Fork, alerts & capacity

Q9

Are many blocked threads as harmful as many runnable ones? What's a rough Fork cost?

A: No — blocked mainly cost stack/metadata. Fork ≈ 2–3 scheduling decisions even with caching.

Q10

SRC alert convention: which threads may you Alert, and why?

A: Only threads you forked — treat Alert as a request that the target terminate completely.

Build order: useful → correct → live → efficient. Measure conflicts; don't optimize away safety.
