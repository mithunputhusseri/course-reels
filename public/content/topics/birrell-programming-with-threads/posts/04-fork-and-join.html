Fork and Join

Fork(proc, arg) starts a new thread at proc. The thread dies when that procedure returns.

t := Fork(a, x);   (* a(x) starts now *)
p := b(y);         (* overlaps with a *)
q := Join(t);      (* wait; q := result of a *)
  • Join waits and returns the initial procedure's result
  • At SRC, Join was uncommon — daemons, no result, or other sync
  • Unjoined finished threads quietly evaporate
Pattern: fork real parallel work; don't fork tasks smaller than ~2–3 scheduling decisions of overhead.
