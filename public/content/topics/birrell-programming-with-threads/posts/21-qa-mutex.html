Mutexes & invariants

Q3

In unprotected Insert, what goes wrong if A and B both execute store then both increment?

A: One value is overwritten (lost); two increments leave an unused NIL slot.

Q4

When must a thread restore the mutex invariant?

A: Before unlocking, and before Wait (because Wait unlocks the mutex).

Check: invariant true whenever the mutex is free; may be temporarily false inside the critical section.
