Three classic designs

Monolithic Modular Microkernel
  • Monolithic — major services in one large kernel (classic Unix, early Linux)
  • Modular — core kernel + loadable modules with defined interfaces (modern Linux LKMs)
  • Microkernel — tiny kernel; most services as user-space servers (Mach, QNX, MINIX 3, L4)
